Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I change my profile picture?
    Answer: Go to your profile settings and click on the "Change Profile Picture" button. You can then select a new image from your computer.
  • What are the privacy settings?
    Answer: Our privacy settings allow you to control who can see your posts, profile information, and more. You can adjust these settings in your account privacy section.
  • How do I report a user?
    Answer: To report a user, go to their profile and look for the 'Report' button (usually three dots or a flag icon). Follow the on-screen instructions to submit your report.
  • How do I delete my account?
    Answer: Deleting your account is permanent. To proceed, go to your account settings, find the "Delete Account" option, and follow the verification steps.
  • Where can I find help?
    Answer: You can find help in our comprehensive FAQ section or contact our support team via email at support@example.com.
